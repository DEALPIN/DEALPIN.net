What Dealpin is

Dealpin records a deal between a seller and a buyer. It does not hold money, deliver goods, judge disputes or guarantee either side. Payments happen outside Dealpin; the app only records what the buyer and seller report.

Receipts and ratings

A receipt shows what the people in a deal reported and the steps recorded. It is a record, not a certificate that goods were good or that money was paid.
