Privacy policy

Last updated 4 November 2026

Who we are

Dealpin is a tool that records deals between sellers and buyers: what was agreed, where it goes, who confirmed what and when. This policy explains what Dealpin stores and who can see it. Contact: dealpin.net@gmail.com.

What we collect

Sign-in: if you sign in with Google, we receive your email address, name and profile picture address from Google. If you sign in by email or text message, we receive that email address or phone number.

Seller profile: the name, link name, phone number and payment details you enter.

Deals: item, price, delivery type, photos, the buyer’s name, phone number, delivery pin or landmark, voice note, payment reference, ratings, disputes and the time of each step. Location and microphone are used only when you tap the button that asks for them.

Technical: your address on the internet is used briefly to limit abuse. Crash reports, if switched on, carry no names, phone numbers, links or deal contents.

Why we use it

To run the service: to sign you in, to show each person the part of a deal they need, to issue receipts and to protect against abuse. We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

Who can see it

The seller, the buyer and the rider each see the parts of a deal they need. A receipt shows the item, price, steps and receipt code, and never the buyer’s phone number or location. A seller’s public page lists verified deals, ratings and dispute counts. Anyone who holds a buyer or rider link can open that part of the deal, so send links only to the person they are for.

Service providers

We use Supabase to store data and run sign-in, Cloudflare to host the app, Google for “Continue with Google”, and an email or text message provider to send sign-in codes. They process data only to provide those services.

Keeping and deleting data

Data is kept while your account exists. In Settings you can download your data or delete your account and everything in it. A deal that already has a buyer is part of both people’s record and cannot be deleted on its own while the account exists.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections, access is limited by rules in the database, and deal changes are made by the server. No system is perfectly secure, so keep your sign-in and links private.

Children

Dealpin is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes we will update the date above. Questions or requests about your data: dealpin.net@gmail.com.
